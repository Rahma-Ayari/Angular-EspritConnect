Birthday Email

Automatically sends an email on users' birthdays.

{{ settings.active ? 'Enabled' : 'Disabled' }} Dernier envoi : {{ settings.lastRunAt | date:'dd MMM yyyy' }}

The email will be sent daily at this time for users whose birthday it is.

Utilisez {{nom}} to insert the recipient's first name.

Comment ça fonctionne
  • The scheduler checks daily at the configured time.
  • Users whose birthday it is receive the email.
  • Utilisez {{nom}} in the template to personalize.
  • Disable the toggle to pause sends.
